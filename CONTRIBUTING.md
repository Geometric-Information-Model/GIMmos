# Contributing to GIMmos

This repository is the central repo for GIM Managed Open Source. Pull requests
are welcome from DMI members and from contributors outside DMI. DMI, as a
partner and client/customer, manages GIMmos and sets the roadmap and policy.
Technical maintainers review proposals and execute merge decisions according
to that roadmap. Existing [`CODEOWNERS`](CODEOWNERS) remains authoritative
for required review.

DMI is not the IP owner or licensor of GIMtransfer. The owner is the
licensor and copyright holder for covered GIM source.

## Before submitting

- Read [`LICENSE`](LICENSE), [`EXCEPTIONS.md`](EXCEPTIONS.md),
  and [`CONTRIBUTOR_TERMS.md`](CONTRIBUTOR_TERMS.md).
- Confirm that you have personal or employer authority to submit every
  contribution.
- Keep third-party material under its own licence and document it in
  [`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
- Do not submit restricted DMI specifications, branding, or proprietary
  material without authorization.

## Terms acceptance

Submitting a pull request or other contribution to this repository constitutes
acceptance of [`CONTRIBUTOR_TERMS.md`](CONTRIBUTOR_TERMS.md). DMI members and
outside participants are contributors under these terms. No copyright
assignment is required. Contributors retain ownership while licensing their
contributions under EUPL v1.2 and the same independent-module permission.

EUPL obligations apply when covered works are distributed or communicated.
EUPL does not require a contribution to be sent back to this repository.

## Review

Describe the change and the intended result in the pull request. Keep changes
focused, preserve documented component boundaries, and include relevant
validation. Maintainers may request changes, defer work, or decline a pull
request according to the DMI-stewarded roadmap and compatibility.
